Common questions

What documents do I need to start?

Usually your KYC, business proof, last two to three years’ financials and ITRs if you are already operating, and quotations for any machinery or assets you plan to buy. We confirm the exact list after a short discussion.

Can a new business with no track record get a loan?

Yes, many schemes and lenders support new ventures. A strong project report with realistic projections and your own contribution makes the case easier to assess.

Do you guarantee loan approval?

No. The lender decides on sanction, amount and terms. Our job is to present your proposal accurately and completely.

How long does the process take?

Preparing the report typically takes a few days once documents are in hand. Sanction timelines depend on the lender and the scheme.
